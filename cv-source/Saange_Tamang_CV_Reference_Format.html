Research Experience

Graph Learning and Reinforcement Learning for Cellular Handover
2026

First author and lead technical implementer; co-author: Sulav Kandel.

• Built a Python cellular-network simulator and PyTorch graph-attention dueling Double-DQN controller using a 46-cell graph, 15 features per cell, and action masking.

• Designed matched comparisons of three frozen policies against a configured LTE Event A3-style baseline across 639 reserved Kathmandu-area cases and 540 Pokhara transfer groups; used paired cluster-bootstrap intervals.

• Kathmandu-area simulations showed 8.86-9.99% higher aggregate throughput and 36.1-53.0% fewer handovers per hour, with a higher share of short completed stays.

Real road geometry; simulated radio and traffic; synthetic Pokhara cell layouts. Operator drive-test logs were unavailable. Shared manuscript preparation equally; Kandel gathered motivating observations and checked site coordinates.
